# Story

Rootopolis is a city of clever, high-tech mice living in the roots of a giant tree. Inventor **Tink Sprocket** and her clockwork beetle **Bolt** fix the city's machines after Grackle the Crow knocks the Grand Gear loose before the Sunset Festival. Each chapter teaches one gear idea in plain words for ages 8+.

## Cast
- **Tink Sprocket**: a young mouse inventor who counts every tooth.
- **Bolt**: her clockwork beetle. He speaks in beeps (subtitled).
- **Mayor Whiskerton**: the fussy mayor.
- **Grackle the Crow**: a clumsy crow who caused the mess and says sorry at the end.

## Prologue
- **Narrator:** Under the roots of a giant oak lies Rootopolis — the cleverest mouse city in the world.
- **Narrator:** Every lift, tram and sprinkler runs on gears, all powered by the mighty Grand Gear.
- **Grackle the Crow:** CAW! Ooh, shiny! I'll just borrow this little bolt… and this one… and — whoops.
- **Narrator:** CLANG. The Grand Gear wobbled loose. All across the city, machines spun too fast, too slow, or backwards.
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** Disaster! The Great Sunset Festival is TONIGHT. Who can fix our gears?!
- **Tink Sprocket:** Me! Tink Sprocket, inventor, gear-fixer, and part-time cheese tester.
- **Bolt:** (Tink: "Bolt says he's ready." Bolt actually said: "Is there snacks?")

## Chapter 1 · Root Docks · The Cheese Lift

### Intro
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** The Root Docks cheese lift is going SO fast the cheese keeps flying off the top!
- **Tink Sprocket:** Easy fix. Here's the big gear secret: when a gear with FEW teeth turns a gear with MANY teeth, the big one turns SLOWER.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Count the teeth. 12 teeth turning 24 teeth? The big one only goes half as fast. 12 ÷ 24 = ½.
- **Bolt:** (Translation: "So small gear on the motor = slow lift. Big gear on the motor = zoomy lift.")
- **Tink Sprocket:** Exactly! We call that the gear RATIO: driver teeth ÷ driven teeth. Let's save the cheese.

### Outro
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** Slow, fast, and just right! The cheese has arrived safely. Mostly.
- **Bolt:** (Bolt is "testing" the cheese. For science.)
- **Tink Sprocket:** Did you see? Two touching gears ALWAYS spin opposite ways. That's going to matter in Trunk Town…

## Chapter 2 · Trunk Town · The Backwards Tram

### Intro
- **Narrator:** Trunk Town. The tram is going backwards. The passengers are going "AAAAAH".
- **Tink Sprocket:** When two gears touch, they spin opposite directions. Clockwise turns the next one anticlockwise.
- **Tink Sprocket:** So to make something spin the SAME way as the motor, slip one extra gear in the middle. We call it an IDLER.
- **Bolt:** (Translation: "An idler is a gear that does no work. Like Bolt on a Sunday.")
- **Tink Sprocket:** And the best part: an idler doesn't change the speed. It only flips the direction.

### Outro
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** Forwards! The tram goes forwards! I could kiss you. I won't. But I could.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Remember: count the flips. Even number of gears in a row = opposite way. Odd number = same way.
- **Grackle the Crow:** Caw… I definitely did not just drop a bolt in the greenhouse pipes. Nope.

## Chapter 3 · Moss Greenhouses · The Greenhouse Sprinklers

### Intro
- **Narrator:** The Moss Greenhouses. One sprinkler is a waterfall. One is a sad little drip.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Here's a trick: one motor can drive LOTS of gears at once — like branches on a tree.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Each branch does its own maths. A big gear makes its sprinkler slow, a small one makes it fast.
- **Bolt:** (Bolt has an umbrella. Bolt is prepared.)

### Outro
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** Every plant is happy! Even the cactus, and he's NEVER happy.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Next stop: the Sap Works. Those pumps need to be REALLY slow… slower than any two gears can do.
- **Bolt:** (Translation: "Sounds sticky.")

## Chapter 4 · Sap Works · The Sap Works Pumps

### Intro
- **Narrator:** The Sap Works, where golden tree sap is pumped up to the whole city.
- **Tink Sprocket:** New trick: put TWO gears on the SAME peg, stacked like a sandwich. They spin together as one — a COMPOUND gear.
- **Tink Sprocket:** The bottom gear slows things down once. The top gear passes that slow speed to the next gear, which slows it AGAIN.
- **Tink Sprocket:** So ÷3 and then ÷3 again is ÷9. The ratios MULTIPLY!
- **Bolt:** (Bolt has stepped in sap. Bolt lives here now.)

### Outro
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** The sap flows! Pancakes for everyone at the Festival!
- **Tink Sprocket:** Compound gears let us go super slow or super fast. Perfect for the Tower Clock…
- **Grackle the Crow:** Caw. I may have also… bumped the clock. With my face. By accident.

## Chapter 5 · Canopy Observatory · The Tower Clock

### Intro
- **Narrator:** High in the Canopy Observatory, the Tower Clock hands are spinning like a helicopter.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Clocks need BIG slow-downs. Too big for one pair of gears. So we chain stages: ÷3, then ÷4…
- **Tink Sprocket:** Multiply them: 3 × 4 = 12 times slower. Add more stages to go even slower!
- **Bolt:** (Bolt's eyes have gone spirally. He watched the clock too long.)

### Outro
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** Tick… tock… perfect time! The Festival starts at sunset — and that's in ONE HOUR!
- **Grackle the Crow:** Caw… okay, okay. It was me. I knocked the Grand Gear loose. I just like shiny things. I'm sorry.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Then help us fix it, Grackle! We'll need every trick we've learned.

## Chapter 6 · Festival Square · The Grand Gear

### Intro
- **Narrator:** Festival Square. The whole city is waiting. The Grand Gear sits silent.
- **Tink Sprocket:** Everything together now: ratios for speed, idlers for direction, compound gears to multiply.
- **Grackle the Crow:** And I promise not to touch anything. Caw. Much.
- **Bolt:** (Translation: "LET'S GOOOO!")

### Outro
- **Narrator:** The Grand Gear turned — slow, steady and strong. Lights flickered on across Rootopolis.
- **Mayor Whiskerton:** Tink Sprocket, you have saved the Great Sunset Festival! You get the Golden Cog… and a lifetime of cheese.
- **Grackle the Crow:** Caw! I brought back all the bolts. And a spoon. The spoon is not yours, I just like it.
- **Bolt:** (Translation: "Best. Day. Ever.")
- **Tink Sprocket:** Thanks, gear-fixer. Come build anything you like in the Sandbox — the workshop is always open!
